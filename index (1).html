Galileo vs Starlink: the Toulouse sky in real time

An interactive 3D globe comparing Europe's Galileo navigation constellation (MEO, ~23,200 km) with SpaceX's Starlink (LEO, ~550 km), as seen from Toulouse. Orbits come from live CelesTrak data, propagated in the browser with SGP4.

  • Switch the view between the ECEF (Earth-fixed) and ICRF (inertial) reference frames
  • Live azimuth/elevation sky plot for Toulouse with a 10° elevation mask
  • Lines of sight to every visible satellite, plus a count of how many are in view
  • CesiumJS
  • SGP4 · satellite.js
  • CelesTrak GP
  • ECEF ↔ ICRF
  • GNSS / PNT

About

I'm a Professional Engineer (P.Eng, British Columbia) and Simon Fraser University MBA based in Bordeaux, France. My career has been about one question: how do we know it works?

I started in aerospace propulsion maintenance, repair and overhaul, moved to validating naval combat systems, and today lead verification & validation for a high-voltage traction battery programme at Automotive Cell Company (ACC). Along the way I've run requirements-to-evidence traceability through PDR, CDR and FDR gates, and built SIL, HIL and flatsat-style test environments.

I'm now steering that experience toward the space sector: satellite navigation, orbital mechanics and PNT. The projects here are part of that journey.
